# SmartHome Simulator

A Unity based VR Simulator that helps to experience how a SmartHome helps and what where it is useful. 

### Different Fields at work

- VR
- 3d modeling
- Configuration of SmartHome devices
- Placement of SmartHome devices

### Usecases

Our project serves the purpose of being able to experience how having a SmartHome would feel without having to first purchase and setup the whole system. It offers similar customizability as a real SmartHome without having the same hastle of setting it up.

## Target Audience

The application is aimed at **people who are considering setting up a smart home and want to experience and test its possibilities before implementing one in their own home**.

Users can **control devices, configure settings, and experiment with different automations** in a virtual environment without having to own or install any real smart home devices.

They can, for example, experience how devices respond to **time, weather conditions, or other triggers**, and see the effects of different configurations on the virtual home.

### Primary Target Audience

**People who are interested in smart home technology and want to experience how different smart home devices, controls, and automations work in practice before setting up a smart home themselves.**

The target audience includes both **people with little or no previous smart home experience** and people who already have some knowledge of smart home technology but want to explore different possibilities before implementing a system in their own home.

### Purpose of the Application

The application provides users with a **realistic virtual environment in which they can experience and test a smart home before setting one up themselves**.

This allows users to explore:

* which smart home devices could be useful for them
* how these devices can be controlled
* which automations are possible
* how different conditions affect the behavior of the devices
* what it could feel like to use a smart home in their everyday life

The application therefore serves as a **virtual testing environment for a potential smart home**.
